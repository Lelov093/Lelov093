## About Me

I am primarily interested in **LLM-based Agents / Agentic AI**, with a long-term focus on intelligent agents that can operate continuously, accumulate experience, make autonomous decisions, interact naturally with humans and other agents, and continuously adapt or evolve over time.

My main interests include:

- **Agent Memory & Experience Learning**
- **Long-Horizon Planning & Decision Making**
- **Continual / Lifelong Learning**
- **Self-Evolving Agents**
- **Multi-Agent Systems & Social Intelligence**
- **Tool Use & Agent-Environment Interaction**

I am also interested in supporting areas that may contribute to more capable agents:

**Machine Learning · Deep Learning · Reinforcement Learning · World Models · Multimodal AI · Affective Computing · LLM Post-training · AIGC**

## Long-term Vision

### JARVIS / FRIDAY-like Personal AI

One of my long-term goals is to explore personal AI agents similar in spirit to **JARVIS / FRIDAY** — intelligent agents that can understand users over long periods of time, maintain meaningful memory, perceive context, autonomously plan and act, use tools, learn from experience, and continuously adapt.

### SAO-like AI-Native Worlds

Another long-term goal is to participate in building **SAO-like AI-native virtual worlds**, where digital characters are not merely scripted NPCs, but autonomous agents capable of remembering experiences, developing relationships, making decisions, learning, growing, and evolving together with players, other agents, and the world itself.

I am also interested in the long-term development of **AGI**. At the current stage, I prefer to approach this broader vision through concrete problems in agents, memory, learning, planning, interaction, and autonomous intelligence.
